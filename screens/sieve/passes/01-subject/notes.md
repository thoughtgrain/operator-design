# Sieve · subject pass 01 · notes

Five sketches of the rest frame (defaults: mesh 2nd, shake 0, keep 83 ms, mix 100 %), all from `sketch.py`, all lint 0 errors / 0 warnings, judged at 1:1. Uniqueness: every pair differs in pattern, language and wording (subject overlap ≤ 0.06); state ready_for_pick. The pick is the owner's.

**v1 · P1 Section drawing · orthographic elevation.** A riddle seen side-on; the input's harmonics fall as rods onto its plate and only the rods over a hole drop through.
Strong: the machine's rule is the drawing; at 2nd you can count every other rod falling through without a legend. Weak: sixteen uprights at 15 px pitch edge toward a bar chart, and the bypass gate for mix is invented, with no twin in a real riddle.

**v2 · P2 Diagram + trace · survey plan.** A surveyed plan of the sieve (numbered stations, open rings or crosses) beside a long-section profile of the output spectrum.
Strong: plan and profile line up with encoder columns 1–2 and 3–4, and the open stations read instantly. Weak: two zones, so neither is plainly the subject; the profile looks like an analyser, and mix shows nothing at its default.

**v3 · P3 Trace as subject · patent section.** Fig. 1: the sieved wave emerging from a hatched wall in section and ringing out between dashed envelope lines, with reference numerals.
Strong: the calmest and most authored of the five, one bright line with sparing patent apparatus; mesh draws the pitch the sieve leaves. Weak: close to "oscilloscope"; at 8th the trace needs 2–3 of the 4 paths, and keep also reshapes the visible trace through its envelope.

**v4 · P4 Stack in depth · hidden-line isometric.** An exploded column of eight round test sieves receding up-right, with the tray at the current mesh lit and holding the kept harmonics as grains.
Strong: the most recognisable object, and mesh as a lit tray stepping down the stack is a large, clear move. Weak: it says "sieve" more than "harmonics"; eight trays is past the pattern's 5–6 cap, and the grains are small at 1:1.

**v5 · P11 Instrument stack · star atlas (stretch).** An atlas plate of a constellation drawn as an organ rank, a star at each pipe mouth, the kept partials bright, named and joined.
Strong: the organ is a real exception case (mutation and mixture stops are harmonic sieves), and the atlas labelling makes it look authored. Weak: it reads as an instrument that makes sound, not an effect acting on one; two controls share scale on star sizes; it only covers h1–h12. My view: it doesn't really fit the category.

## Ranking

1. v1: the rule (keep every Nth) is visible at a glance and every control has a physical part.
2. v3: best drawing and the calmest screen, but it shows the result rather than the mechanism.
3. v2: clear but split, and it drifts toward an analyser.
4. v4: easy to read as a sieve but says little about the sound.
5. v5: an honest stretch that doesn't quite fit an effect.
