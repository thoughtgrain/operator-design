**v1 · P15 cell field · hidden-line isometric.** The bowl's glazed floor as a flat isometric slab-tile field: wireframe tiles, grey glazed runs, a bright lit patch at the strike point.
Strong: crisp at 1:1, one bright clump, hidden-line edges hold up. Weak: it isn't a bowl, Gate 0 argues against a cell field for a synth with a mechanism, strike and glaze both act on cell counts, and 224 nodes at rest leaves no headroom.

**v2 · P1 section drawing · star atlas.** The bowl cut a–a as one bright U-section on a foot ring, glaze pooled in the well, modes n2–n7 charted as stars along an ecliptic, the mallet a meteor trail from its radiant.
Strong: the bowl is unmistakable and the brightest thing; every control moves something physical (trail angle, glaze level, a fissure and double stars, star magnitudes); cheap (9 nodes). Weak: two languages (section + chart) must be held together; upper stars are ~2 px at rest, under the part floor; the glaze line is faint at 30.

**v3 · P2 diagram + trace · engineering schematic.** Mallet impulse → four resonator boxes with a shared glaze dashpot → summing junction, ring trace on the right; knobs 1–2 drive the left, 3–4 the right.
Strong: explains how the sound is made, column split matches the hands, trace is the only bright thing. Weak: boxes, a slider-like dashpot and a tick ruler read as a panel; fire's ruler ticks are thin; the transient is jagged and the trace needs more than one 64-point path.

**v4 · P3 trace as subject · oscilloscope.** The bowl's ring big on a 10 × 8 graticule, the high mode alone on channel 2, a −40 dB cursor where glaze kills it.
Strong: the clearest control readings (amplitude, cursor, beating, channel-2 period) and a calm, legible frame. Weak: P3's own pitfall, an oscilloscope that says nothing about ceramic; channel 1 needs 3–4 mutable paths at this cycle count.

**v5 · P12 orrery (stretch) · typeset diagram.** The bowl in plan as the sun, modes n = 2…5 as orbits, 2n antinodes as planets, labelled like a textbook figure.
Strong: physically honest (a bowl's modes are standing waves round the rim; a crack's doublets beat as their nodes travel), handsome, layered in 3D, cheap. Weak: no sequence, so the orrery is static between notes and its link/flash machinery has nothing to do; glaze and fire both on scale; labels crowd the right side.
