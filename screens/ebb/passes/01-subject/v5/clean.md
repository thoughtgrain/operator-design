- I removed all seven figures: +0, +12, +24, +36 on the left, and -16s, -8s, "now" under the chart. Each one became a small tick outside the neat line in the atlas's own hairline: one per octave up the left side, and one each at -16 s and -8 s along the bottom. In each column the brighter star now wins, and a fainter one that would come within 2 px of it, or of the "now" star, is left out. The field's rule (size from how near the tide passes) is unchanged.
- I redrew the ecliptic's dashes at 5 px with 4 px gaps. The sketch's 3 px dashes fell under the legibility floor.
- Values and weights: the stars are solid bodies in one mid grey (`#969696`), sized by magnitude, never shaded by state. The "now" star is the only `#CDCDCD`. The neat line, graticule, ticks and ecliptic are all Hairline except the "now" tick.
