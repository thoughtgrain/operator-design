# Pylon · subject pass 01 · notes

All five sketches are `v*/rest.svg` from `sketch.py`, lint 0 errors / 0 warnings, judged at 1:1 from `v*/rest.png`.

**v1 · P10 voice field, patent section.** Five rows of hidden-line slabs (one per conductor) whose heights are the wire's standing wave, between two cross-arms cut in hatched section, with patent numerals.
Strong: the model is the drawing, and every control acts on it the way it acts on the sound. Weak at 1:1: the field reads as scattered blocks before it reads as loops along a wire; it's toro's pattern, and at full span it's ~270 nodes.

**v2 · P15 cell field, hidden-line isometric.** An isometric grid downwind of one bright wire, with raised cells as vortex rolls.
Strong: the calmest, cleanest frame at 1:1, and span as spanwise correlation is real physics with a clear run-length channel. Weak: it draws the wind, not the singing, so the wire's modes (the sound) never appear, and Gate 0 says a machine with a mechanism should draw the mechanism.

**v3 · P1 section drawing, star atlas.** The span on a sky chart: two pylon stars, the wire a chain of standing-wave loops on its catenary, the graticule behind.
Strong: the loop chain is one bright subject that says "standing wave" with no legend, and wind (loops) and span (pylons apart, sag) both read at a glance. Weak: it's an elevation, not a section; the background stars and antinode stars sit close in grey to the subject and need thinning.

**v4 · P2 diagram + trace, engineering schematic.** A shedding source taps a ladder of LC mode rungs (left); the sum with a mains line feeds the output trace (right).
Strong: explains the machine fully and lines up with the encoder columns (structure 1–2, trace 3–4). Weak at 1:1: reads as a circuit diagram any synth could have, with no pylon or wire on screen; 12 rungs crowd the coil symbols.

**v5 · P14 loom, oscilloscope trace (stretch).** Four rows of the wire's recent shapes receding on a graticule, warp threads through the nodes.
Strong: gust is the one control whose effect is change over time, and rows that agree or disagree show it in a still. Weak: mostly doesn't fit. There are no steps, the rest frame is a memory, it reads as a persistence scope (P3's pitfall), and it needs five mutable outlines against a limit of four.

## Ranking

1. **v3**: the clearest picture of what the sound is (a wire singing in loops), one bright subject, lots of black.
2. **v1**: the most faithful mapping and the richest at 1:1, but has to shed its toro resemblance and its node bill.
3. **v4**: explains everything but looks like a panel.
4. **v2**: beautiful and calm, but of the wrong thing.
5. **v5**: an honest stretch that doesn't earn the exception.

The pick is the owner's.
