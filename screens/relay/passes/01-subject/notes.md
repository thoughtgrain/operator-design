# Relay · subject pass 01 · notes

All five drawn by `sketch.py` at the defaults (stages 1000 / 100 ms, leak 0, clock 0, feedback 35 %), linted to 0 errors and 0 warnings, judged as `rest.png` at 1:1. Submitted: `ready_for_pick`, all ten pairs differ in pattern and language, subject overlap ≤ 0.11.

**v1 · P4 Stack in depth · oscilloscope trace.** Four stacked scope sweeps, one per trip round the chain, with the echo burst bright on the front sweep and dimmer and smaller on each one behind.
Strong: it reads at once at 1:1, feedback becomes a count of layers and stages slides the burst along every sweep, and both match what you hear. Weak: it's close to "oscilloscope", stages and clock are both position, and at defaults nothing looks like it's failing.

**v2 · P10 Voice field · typeset diagram.** The chain coiled into a spiral of radial bucket bars, each turn one more recirculation, with the note as a lump of long bars at about five o'clock.
Strong: it's the most original idea, and the spiral is feedback without needing a legend. Weak: at 1:1 the bars are thin and the lump is small; the inner turns sit near the 3 px hatching floor, and when clock moves every bar it blows the redraw budget.

**v3 · P15 Cell field · orthographic elevation.** A wall of compartments seen face on, the chain running back and forth along the rows. Every compartment holds a dark bias charge, and runs of bright, fuller compartments are the echoes of a test phrase.
Strong: it reads as texture, and feedback (density), leak (drained cells) and clock (run length) are true field statistics. Weak: at 1:1 it looks like a bank of level meters, a BBD has a mechanism so Gate 0 argues against P15, and stages as spacing between runs is a weak mapping.

**v4 · P1 Section drawing · survey plan.** The charge channel cut open lengthwise as a long-section: clock gates over sixteen wells, a bright pool surface in each tracing the note over a bias level, a datum chained 0–1000 in stages, and a return leg with its grade line underneath.
Strong: it looks the most authored of the five and the most like this machine, and leak (sinking pools) and clock (uneven gates and wells) show the failure directly. Weak: it explains the device more than the sound, carries a lot of labels for 480 × 220, and feedback as a grade line is a quiet mark for a score-4 control.

**v5 · P11 Instrument stack (stretch) · patent section.** The name taken literally: a patent drawing of tipping buckets on a sloped frame, with the note's bucket tipped and pouring bright and a riser carrying the overflow back to the top.
Strong: people recognise it straight away and the patent numerals hold it together; clock as uneven tilt is a strong rotation. Weak: it's a stretch that barely fits. Nobody plays buckets, so P11's reason (an instrument you already know by sight) only holds as a pun. It's close to clip-art, and stages as four to ten buckets can't show delay time.

## Ranking (mine; the pick is the owner's)

1. v4: the strongest drawing and the most specific to this machine
2. v1: the clearest mapping of control to sound
3. v2: the best idea, but it needs room it doesn't have at 1:1
4. v5: charming and readable, but a stretch that doesn't really fit
5. v3: reads as meters, and Gate 0 argues against it
