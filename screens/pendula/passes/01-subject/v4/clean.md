# pendula v4 · cleanup

Same orrery of conical pendulums, same camera and composition as `rest.svg`. Generated by `clean.py` → `clean.svg`, `clean.png`, copied to `screens/pendula/frames/rest.svg` / `rest.png` as the pick. Lint: 0 errors, 0 warnings.

- I removed every piece of text: the reference numerals 10, 12, 14 and their leaders, "FIG. 1", and both section letters "A". The cutting-plane line stays as a drawn mark, with heavier ends past the rim and long dashes across the plate. That's how a section reads without letters.
- The bobs touched each other in a chain of rings. I spread the rods a little more (shortest now 36 % of the longest instead of 45 %) and sized each bob from the measured spacing, so there are at least 3 px of black between neighbours. They're now solid bright bodies (the subject's one bright mass, `#CDCDCD`). A 2 px ring at that size left only a pinhole, which read as noise.
- I redrew the hub's section hatching as true 45° hairlines, 3.5 px apart, clipped to the hub and kept off its outline. In the sketch it was four slanted strokes under 3 px apart.
- I brought line weights onto the table. The cutting-plane ends were 2.5 px (a Subject weight on a secondary mark) and are now 1.5. Rod, spindle, rim and long dial ticks are 1.5, and orbits, dashes and short ticks are Hairline.
- Nothing crowds the edges any more. I lengthened the dial ticks so the foreshortened front ones stay over 3 px (the sketch had three parts under the legibility floor), moved the whole figure down 3 px so the hub clears the header by 11 px, and shortened the cutting-plane ends so the drawing ends at y 206.
