# Flock · subject pass 01 · notes

Five rest frames from `sketch.py`, one flock model (eight birds, seeded) at the defaults: cohere 50 %, speed 1.00, scatter 0, scale pent. All lint at 0 errors; v2 has four warnings (1.0 px stroked orbits and title block, hairlines by craft.md) and v5 two (a short dash piece). Judged at 1:1 from `vN/rest.png`.

**v1 · P5 field and figure · star atlas.** The pitch field as a conic atlas sheet (meridians = scale degrees, parallels = loudness, `c3 c4 c5` on the octave meridians) with the flock as one asterism, proper-motion vectors for speed, a raised-mark strip for what's sounding.
Strong: reads at once as a chart with a group of stars on it, every control has an obvious place, the field is the machine's own pitch field. Weak at 1:1: the stars are small (3–5 px) and the grid nearly competes with them; the strip leans toward a meter.

**v2 · P12 orrery · engineering schematic.** Birds on four orbits round a centre of gravity, a graduated two-octave rim, dashed leaders from each bird to the pitch at its bearing, a sector dimension for cohere.
Strong: the drafting reads (rim ticks, leaders, dimension arc, title block) and cohere as a dimensioned sector is clear. Weak at 1:1: busy with hairlines, the rim touches the bottom of the body, pitch-as-bearing has a seam at c3/c5, and birds don't orbit anything: the metaphor is borrowed, not the flock's.

**v3 · P13 capture · oscilloscope trace.** A storage scope: each bird's pitch over the last five beats as a stepped trace, older ends further out, pulled between two dashed capture cursors (the cohesion band, `Δ 11 st`) by "now".
Strong: the convergence into the band shows what cohesion does to the notes without a legend, and speed as risers per trace is exact. Weak at 1:1: eight crossing traces are busy in the middle divisions, and at high speed this becomes hatching; scrolling history is expensive on the device.

**v4 · P14 loom · typeset diagram.** Four bars woven as cloth: 11 warp threads (the scale's pitches), one row per bar receding, slabs where birds played, front row solid, row numbers and a figure caption.
Strong: history is visible (the cluster holds on the same threads bar to bar) and it looks like a book figure. Weak at 1:1: reads first as a 3D bar chart, and no bird is on screen, so the machine's own image is missing.

**v5 · P11 instrument stack · orthographic elevation (stretch).** Birds perched on telegraph wires in front elevation, one wire per scale degree; a perched bird sounds its wire, birds aloft are hopping.
Strong: instantly legible and charming, and birds-on-a-wire already reads as notes on a staff. Weak at 1:1: the silhouettes sit near the icon line, the poles are scenery, chr (25 wires, 5 px apart) breaks the stack-spacing floor, and P11's reason (a known instrument) doesn't really apply to a player.

## Ranking (mine; the pick is the owner's)

1. v1: the cleanest fit of pattern, language and machine; every control moves part of one picture.
2. v3: the most explanatory of cohesion, at the cost of density and redraw.
3. v5: the most immediate image, but a stretch with icon and spacing risks.
4. v4: good history, but no birds.
5. v2: the orrery is a stand-in the flock doesn't share.
