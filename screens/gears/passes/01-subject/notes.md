# Gears · subject pass 01 · notes

Sketches: `vN/rest.svg` (+ `rest.png`, rsvg-convert at 480 × 220 on black), all from `sketch.py`, defaults (3:2, 8 teeth, mesh 100 %, maj), no notes. Each lints at 0 errors; the warnings are the dot segments of dash-dot centre lines (v1, v4) and the 2 px dither cells (v5), all under the 3 px part floor. Pass state: ready_for_pick (all ten pairs differ in pattern and language, subject overlap ≤ 0.10).

**v1 · P14 Loom · engineering schematic.** A's last four revolutions as rows of cloth, slabs where meshes sounded, B's index tooth a dash-dot line across the rows, small schematic pitch circles of A and B driving it.
Strong: the only one that shows history, and the 3:2 repeat is literally visible (rows 0 and 3 match). Weak at 1:1: busy; back rows crowd behind the front ones, the index line is the faintest thing on screen, and the drive circles are a second picture.

**v2 · P5 Field and figure · oscilloscope trace.** X-Y plot of A's angle against B's: the ratio draws itself as a 3-by-2 Lissajous figure over a dim graticule floor, beads per mesh, a 24-bar note strip.
Strong: reads in one glance, one bright figure, and the ratio control has the most honest shape change of all five. Weak at 1:1: it's the stock image of a scope, and nothing on screen is a gear; beads pile up at self-crossings.

**v3 · P12 Orrery · typeset diagram.** Wheels as two orbits round one arbor, a semitone ring outside with the scale's degrees, two bright planets on the mesh line toward the reader.
Strong: calmest and most OP-XY of the five; the typeset labels make it look authored. Weak at 1:1: the arbor does nothing and orbits round one centre aren't meshing wheels, so the rule is lost; station dots are at the 5 px floor.

**v4 · P13 Capture · orthographic elevation.** Two spur gears in front elevation with centre lines, pitch circles, bores and a centre-distance dimension; tooth length is weight, the contact tooth lit; a plain octave keyboard for the scale.
Strong: the drawing is the rule: mesh is the centre distance, so turning it shows exactly which teeth stop reaching, and mute is B sliding out of mesh. Weak at 1:1: tooth-length differences (4–9 px) only read near the contact point, and the keyboard is bolted on.

**v5 · P9 One-bit ink · survey plan (stretch).** Wheel A as a dithered one-bit plot with numbered lots and a traverse, a two-row ratio scale bar and a stepped scale section beside it.
Strong: crisp, graphic, the glyphs are cleanly one control each. Weak at 1:1: it's the settings-cog icon; only one wheel, so the mesh is gone; every outline is the brightest grey. I don't think it fits.

## Ranking (designer's view; the pick is the owner's)

1. v4: the machine's rule drawn as itself, every control on geometry, a natural muted state.
2. v1: the right idea for a player (history), but needs thinning to read at 1:1.
3. v2: clearest single read, best ratio channel, but it's a scope, not gears.
4. v3: handsome and calm, but the picture doesn't do the machine's job.
5. v5: doesn't fit; icon risk and the mesh is off screen.
