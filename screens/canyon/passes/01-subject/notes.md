# Canyon · subject pass 01 · notes

All five sketches are drawn by `sketch.py` from one echo model at the defaults (distance 253 ms, walls 4, absorb 35 %, wind 0), linted at 0 errors and 0 warnings, and judged as `vN/rest.png` at 1:1. Submitted: `ready_for_pick`, every pair differs in pattern and language, subject overlap ≤ 0.12.

**v1 · P4 Stack in depth · survey plan.** Four contoured rock benches stacked back up a dashed chainage line from a survey station, the nearest bench bright, metres at each end.
Strong: the stack and the chainage make distance and walls legible, and the jagged-to-smooth rims are a real rock reading of absorb. Weak at 1:1: from above the benches look like stacked cushions more than canyon walls, absorb's serration is only a few pixels, and walls past six have to fold away.

**v2 · P10 Voice field · patent section.** The impulse response as a row of hatched columns on a section line, one peak per tap, with numerals 10/12/14 and "fig. 1".
Strong: the model is the drawing, so the tap pattern (spacing, count, slump) reads at a glance, and the patent hatching and ground symbol make it look authored. Weak at 1:1: it sits one step from a bar meter, three controls are all heights, and at short distances the taps pile into a lump.

**v3 · P15 Cell field · hidden-line isometric.** An isometric floor of wire cells where each echo front lights an arc of raised blocks out from the shout's block, later arcs dropping to grey tiles.
Strong: the arcs-going-grey reading of absorb is immediate, and the hidden-line wireframe is crisp. Weak at 1:1: an echo has a mechanism, so a texture field is the weaker answer (Gate 0 / P15 pitfall); the arcs read as a scatter more than as fronts, it costs 200+ nodes, and it sits close to toro's field in look.

**v4 · P1 Section drawing · star atlas.** The canyon cut across, walls as the dark limb over a hairline graticule, the echoes plotted as lettered stars of falling magnitude on a constellation line zig-zagging up from the shout.
Strong: it reads as a canyon and as an echo at once, every control moves a physical thing (walls apart, stars added, magnitudes, drift), and the lettering makes it look like a chart someone drew. Weak at 1:1: the graticule is busy behind the stars, the 1 px constellation line is faint, and twelve stars in a narrow canyon will crowd.

**v5 · P5 Field and figure · engineering schematic (stretch).** Two wall blocks in elevation with the gap dimensioned in metres, over a receding floor grid, the taps ticked along a time strip.
Strong: the cleanest frame of the five; the dimension line says "distance" without a legend and the strip is an honest picture of the tap train. Weak at 1:1: the strip carries two controls and reads as a meter under a picture, the walls are plain pillars, and P5 only half fits (the exception is that an echo's output is discrete events in time, which is what P5's strip is for).

## Ranking

1. v4: the strongest single scene, every control physical, most authored.
2. v2: the most self-explaining mapping, at the risk of reading as a meter.
3. v1: the most original composition, but the plan view needs its labels to read as a canyon.
4. v5: clean and clear, but split into picture plus dashboard, and a stretch.
5. v3: wrong pattern for a machine with a mechanism, and the heaviest budget.

The pick is the owner's.
