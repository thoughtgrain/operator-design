# Spool · subject pass 02 · notes

Owner's focus after pass 01 was rejected: draw a spool, thread or yarn wound on a spool, five ways. All five are spools of thread; `definition.json` and the four controls are unchanged (every control could be drawn; the thread stands in for the tape: wear frays or thins it, wow is the spool running untrue, length is how much is wound or laid, bias is tension or packing).

Five rest sketches from `sketch.py`, all at defaults (wear 0, wow 0, length 0.32 s, bias 0). Every spool is modelled in 3D and projected through one camera per frame; v5 removes hidden lines with a depth buffer, the others by painter's order over black fills. Lint: 0 errors on all five, no `text-in-drawing`; v4 and v5 carry only `legibility` warnings (1 px hairlines, small edge fragments). No text below the header, grey only, one #CDCDCD thing per frame (the thread, or the thread's cut sections in v5). Body black at rest: v1 96 %, v2 92 %, v3 96 %, v4 92 %, v5 84 %. `design_pass_submit` → `ready_for_pick`, every pair differs in angle and language, subject overlap ≤ 0.10. I've only drawn the rest frames; none of the control travel described in the candidates has been rendered or measured yet.

**v1 · the trace left behind · single-weight contour.** A spool lying on a table pays out a trail of thread behind it, laid in the written cycle's shape repeat after repeat, receding to the far edge.
At 1:1: the trail recedes convincingly and the idea (the loop's history lying on the table) is this machine's own. Weak: the zigzag edges toward a chart or a scribble, the spool sits small in the corner, and one weight leaves the drawing thin.

**v2 · one detail at large scale · hatched mass.** The flange lip of a big spool cropped by the frame, the wound mass under it hatched as turns of thread, one bright thread peeling off under the rim and out of frame.
At 1:1: the boldest and calmest of the five, with a clear figure, lots of black and an obvious subject in the bright strand. Weak: with the rest cropped away it can read as a lampshade or a drum, and the hatch is costly to re-key when bias changes the turn pitch.

**v3 · the object, literally · edge-lit silhouette.** A wooden spool of thread on end, black on black, its right-hand edges lit from behind, the loose end hanging over the bottom flange and curling on the ground.
At 1:1: instantly a spool of thread, and the tail gives it a gesture. Weak: it's the plainest of the five and small in the frame. The edge light is subtle at this size, so the dim side may disappear on the panel. Wow and bias both act on the tail.

**v4 · the mechanism · detailed line art.** A hand bobbin winder: base plate, bearing posts, spindle and crank, a bobbin filling, the thread coming in through two tension discs on a sprung stud.
At 1:1: the most authored drawing, with good line weights, legible parts and a bright thread that leads the eye from the discs to the bobbin. Every control has a physical part (bobbin fill, crank, tension nut). Weak: it's busy and node-heavy, the wow-to-crank mapping is a stand-in rather than the physics, and the tension parts are small.

**v5 · the material seen close · hidden-line 3D.** A spool on its side with a quarter of the winding cut away, so the thread shows in section: bright round cuts layer on layer on the barrel between two whole flanges.
At 1:1: the closest fit to the sound. Wear, length and bias each become something you can see in the layers, and wow becomes an eccentric reel, which is what wow physically is. Weak: about 330 small outlines, far over budget as drawn, and the cut faces read as a mesh before they read as thread.

## Ranking (mine; the pick is the owner's)

1. **v4**: looks most like a finished OP-XY drawing, and every control moves a real part of the mechanism.
2. **v5**: the closest fit to what spool does, but it needs a budget rethink (rows as mutable outlines).
3. **v2**: striking and calm, the most black, and a clear bright subject, but the crop risks losing "spool".
4. **v3**: right object, drawn well, but too plain and small; edge light is subtle at 1:1.
5. **v1**: the trace idea is apt, but it reads weakest as a picture.
