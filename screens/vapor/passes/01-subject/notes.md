# Vapor · pass 01 subject · notes

All five sketched at defaults (vowel a, breath 25, throat 1.00, glide 60ms) by `sketch.py`, linted at 0 errors (11 warnings, all hairline strokes under 1.5 px and the 2 × 1 px dots of v2's dash-dot centre line), and viewed at 1:1. Uniqueness: `ready_for_pick`, the highest subject overlap is 0.09.

**v1 · P15 Cell field · typeset diagram.** The vowel chart as a field of 20 px cells: a bright clump on the vowel, grey breath haze round it, a trail of cells back to u for glide, and the five vowel letters typeset in place.
Strong: crisp hard edges, and the clump's position on the chart is the clearest vowel reading of the five. Weak: at 1:1 it's mostly empty dots, so it reads close to a screensaver. Breath haze and throat clump size are both cell counts told apart mainly by grey. And it's the wrong pattern by Gate 0, because vapor has a mechanism to draw.

**v2 · P1 Section drawing · orthographic elevation.** The throat as a sixteen-section acoustic tube in elevation from glottis to lips, about a centre line, with a dimension line reading 17.5 cm.
Strong: one bright subject that is the sound's own mechanism. Each control does something physical to it (profile, length, air, transition), and the stepped profile reads as a vowel shape right away. Weak: the glide ghost profiles crowd the subject outline to 2–4 px in places and start to look like hatching. The breath streaks are small and buried in the tube, too quiet for a score of 4.

**v3 · P2 Diagram + trace · survey plan.** The vowel quadrilateral surveyed as a plot (stations, a traverse with chainage ticks to the vowel) on the left, the spectral profile as a long section over a 0–4k datum on the right.
Strong: the profile is a clean, bright, readable curve, and the plan shows exactly where the voice sits and where the glide came from. Weak: two halves split the eye, the knob columns don't match the halves, and there are a lot of labels. The station triangles are close to icons.

**v4 · P3 Trace as subject · patent section.** The pressure wave at the lips: four glottal periods, the first two grey ghosts still gliding out of u, the aspiration noise traced beneath, with patent numerals and FIG. 1.
Strong: the boldest frame at 1:1. The bright ringing is unmistakably a voice, and the grey-to-bright onset explains glide without a word. Weak: it leans toward "oscilloscope", the numerals mean nothing without a legend, and four periods use the whole four-path budget. Glide as a count of four is under the floor.

**v5 · P11 Instrument stack · hidden-line isometric (stretch).** Five vowel resonators stacked on a throat neck, the sung vowel's chamber solid with a bright edge, breath rising off the top as rings.
Strong: the hidden-line drawing is tidy, and the throat neck is a literal, legible length. Weak: nobody knows this instrument by sight, the knob (not the note) picks the chamber, and the silhouette is uncomfortably close to theseus's tower. The stretch doesn't earn its place.

## Ranking (a reviewer's ranking; the pick is the owner's)

1. v2: the mechanism itself, one subject, every control physical
2. v4: the strongest image at 1:1, needs the oscilloscope risk managed
3. v3: the most informative, but split and busy
4. v1: crisp, but the wrong pattern for a machine with a mechanism
5. v5: a stretch that doesn't fit
