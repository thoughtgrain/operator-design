# Flock · subject pass 02 · notes

Five rest frames from `sketch.py`. One flock model (eight birds, seeded) at the defaults: cohere 50 %, speed 1.00, scatter 0, scale pent (11 degrees). Every 3D part is modelled and projected through one camera per frame. The stock header is the template's, unchanged, and there's no other text. All five lint at 0 errors and 0 warnings, with no `text-in-drawing`. Each has exactly one `#CDCDCD` element, and 83–94 % of the body is black. I judged them at 1:1 from `vN/rest.png`. Nothing here repeats pass 01's pitch plans, labelled elevations or bar histories.

**v1 · reed bed cut open · bold closed outlines.** The pitch field is a reed bed tuned as a pan pipe. Each reed is cut open down its front to show the node that stops its air column, so the nodes step down as pitch rises. The bird skimming a mouth blows that reed.
Strong: the inside explains the machine (column length is pitch) and no other machine would have this screen. Weak at 1:1: eleven tall tubes are a lot of repeated outline, the nodes are small, and at chr (25 reeds) the cutaway detail has to go.

**v2 · wing prints in snow · cutaway section.** A block of snowy ploughed field cut open in section, one furrow per degree. It carries the wing prints the flock left wherever a bird touched down, and the newest print is the bright one.
Strong: the calmest and most precise of the five, with lots of black, and the furrows read as a field rather than a chart. Weak at 1:1: the prints at the back are foreshortened and crowd, the flock itself is absent (only its marks), and the history needs a rule so it doesn't turn into a bar history.

**v3 · one bird, exploded · exploded view.** One bird drawn large and pulled apart like a model kit: body, wing arms, primaries (one per degree, low octave on the left wing) and tail. Its neighbours are cut off by the frame's edges.
Strong: striking, and cohere as how far the kit is exploded is a literal picture of the word. Weak at 1:1: one bird stands in for eight voices, the primaries crowd at their roots (worse at chr), and the edge neighbours are large grey shapes that compete a little.

**v4 · starlings over the field · single-weight contour.** Eight starlings seen from above, each in its own wingbeat, over drill rows (one per degree) that run to a headland, with hatched shadows on the rows.
Strong: the birds look like birds, and the flock is the subject without any device-like framing. Weak at 1:1: one weight for every bird leaves the bright bird told apart only by grey, the small hatched shadows are at the limit of reading, and it is the closest of the five to "a flock over a field".

**v5 · crank automaton · hatched mass.** A box with eight carved birds on rods riding a slot over a tuned comb. Each rod's pick plucks the tine under it, and a crank on the end drives it.
Strong: every control is a moving part (the crank for speed, rod spread for cohere, leaning rods for scatter, tine count for scale), and the carved birds are charming. Weak at 1:1: the birds bunch over the same tines and touch, the tines are small, and a comb with a crank leans toward "music box", so the birds have to carry the identity.

## Ranking (mine; the pick is the owner's)

1. v2: closest to how the OP-XY draws (calm, precise, physical). The trace is this machine's alone, and every control moves the prints.
2. v1: explains the pitch field best. The cutaway does real work; it needs thinning for chr.
3. v5: the clearest control mapping and the most charm. Bird overlap and the music-box read are the risks.
4. v3: a strong image, but one bird for eight voices is a stretch.
5. v4: well drawn, but the most generic idea and the weakest bright-element read.
