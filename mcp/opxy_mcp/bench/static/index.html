      MIDI: how the knobs bind

      Connect MIDI (top right) in Chrome or Edge, then turn a knob on your controller. The first knobs you turn bind to the controls in order; to choose, press learn under a control and turn the knob you want. Notes from any channel hold the screen's note state. Bindings are remembered per machine in this browser.

      The OP-XY itself works as the controller over USB if its encoders send CC on your setup; endless encoders that send relative steps want rel switched on for their binding.
