**v1 · P5 · oscilloscope trace.** The pool's ring-down after the last swell as one bright trace over three dim, receding traces of earlier swells, with wave arrivals in the bottom strip.
Strong: the bright trace is the clear brightest thing and reads as "a wave, then ringing" at a glance. Weak at 1:1: the far traces are small and fussy, it's close to a plain scope (P3's warning), and nothing says rock pool; depth and salt both reshape the one trace.

**v2 · P10 · typeset diagram.** A Hinton chart: eight resonator rows across four seconds, squares sized by level, the latest onset column brightest.
Strong: the cleanest and most legible at 1:1; the model is the drawing, and every control has an obvious, separate geometric job. Weak: the least about the sea, and the f1..f8 labels and time axis push it toward a chart or meter grid; swell turns redraw every column.

**v3 · P15 · orthographic elevation.** The shore cut in elevation as a cell grid: hollow rock, grey water, lit foam runs.
Strong: big, crisp, and the shore reads in profile. Weak: it looks like a block game at 20 px cells, the hollow rock is heavy, and P15 is for sounds without a mechanism, which tide isn't.

**v4 · P1 · survey plan.** The pool from above: one bright rim open to the sea, contoured basins with soundings, a dotted tide mark, swell fronts arriving through the mouth.
Strong: the most authored-looking frame; one clear subject, lots of black, labels that belong to the language, and it's unmistakably a rock pool. Weak: depth must be read from contour count (a convention), the soundings are text that wants to change with depth, and the strike on a note still needs designing.

**v5 · P13 · patent section (stretch).** A section through the shore: the swell coming in, the rim it overtops, the pool with three chambers of different depth, numerals and leaders.
Strong: explains the machine (waves in, tuned hollows ringing) in one picture, and the hatched rock gives it weight. Weak: the hatching is dense, the bright water is split into two lines, and as Capture it only half fits: swell is a period, not a threshold, so the rim does nothing a control moves. It's really a P1 drawing with P13's argument.

## Ranking

1. v4: best looking at 1:1 and most clearly a rock pool; controls map to separate channels.
2. v5: clearest about how the machine works, but it's a weak stretch as Capture and the busiest frame.
3. v2: most legible model, least about the sea.
4. v1: reads instantly, but it says little beyond "oscilloscope".
5. v3: crisp, but blocky, and the wrong pattern for a sound that has a mechanism.
