# Bellows · screen brief

## 0 · Screen or stock page?

- Subject in one sentence: a free reed blown from a bellows, where the wind (its pressure, sag and leaks) and the reeds it sounds (one, or two beating) are the object every control acts on.
- Not stock: there's a mechanism with parts each control moves (boards and pleats, tongue, seam, second reed).
