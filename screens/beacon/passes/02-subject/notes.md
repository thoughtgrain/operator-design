# Beacon · subject pass 02 · notes

The owner's focus: a lighthouse, five ways. All five frames come from `sketch.py`, drawn at the defaults (pulse 25, lens 83, sweep 2.4 st, fog 0). The 3D ones (v1, v2, v3, v4) are modelled in world units and projected through one camera each. Every frame lints at 0 errors and 0 warnings, with no text below the header. I judged them at 1:1 from `vN/rest.png`, and checked them at the extremes too (pulse 100, lens 0, sweep 100, fog 60, and pulse 0, lens 100, sweep 0), not saved here. Black share of the body: v1 91 %, v2 93 %, v3 93 %, v4 82 %, v5 93 %. Uniqueness: `ready_for_pick`, with subject overlap at most 0.13.

**v1 · the lens drum, close · edge-lit silhouette.** A beehive drum lens fills the frame, cut off top and bottom. Its prism rings catch the lamp only round the flashing panel and as short rims at the two silhouettes, and the flame's image sits bright in the belt.
Strong: the most striking crop, a lot of black, and every control acts on the lens itself (lens = ring count). Weak: if you don't know a beehive lens it can read as a barrel or lampshade. Sweep redraws every bar and lit arc, and at sweep 100 the flame shrinks to a sliver at the edge.

**v2 · the lighthouse · detailed line art.** A stone tower on a stepped base at the horizon, seen from a low camera: coursed masonry, door, windows, corbelled gallery, railing, helical glazing and dome, with a hatched beam out to sea.
Strong: it reads as a lighthouse instantly at 1:1. It's calm, it sits beside theseus-tower comfortably, and the beam's cone (lens) and turn (sweep) are clear. Weak: it's the most literal and nearest a postcard. The tower is narrow and the lantern detail is at the legibility floor, so pulse (the lamp's width) is small.

**v3 · the clockwork and the turning lens · hidden-line 3D.** A four-panel flashing lens on its mercury float and pedestal, rack teeth round the trough, and a weight clock beside it (great wheel, pinion, fly, cable through the floor). The flash shows in the panel facing you.
Strong: it shows what makes the light turn, and the great wheel gives sweep a cheap true in-plane turn. The hidden-line model is clean and well spaced. Weak: the concentric panel rings sit close to "target rings", though the prism bars help. The clock splits the eye from the lens, and the fly reads as a little flag.

**v4 · the sea it lights · bold closed outlines.** Seen from the waterline: rows of wave crests drawn as closed outlines that hide the ones behind, a glitter path of lit crest edges beneath a small lighthouse on its rock, and a hatched beam swinging over the sky.
Strong: the most atmospheric of the five and the most different. The beam modelled as a cone rises toward you honestly, and the glitter path is a good home for lens and pulse. Weak: the crest field is busy (82 % black) and reads partly as texture, and the tower is small. At the extremes the fog and beam hatching cross into a crosshatch.

**v5 · the headland, the tower cut open · cutaway section.** A headland at night with the tower in section (walls, stair treads, floors, the clock weight in its shaft in the rock, the lens profile round the lamp), and a beam carrying hatched bands of light out over the sea.
Strong: the beam's bands are the pulse train itself (pulse = band width as a share of the period), the most model-true mapping in the pass. The scene is clear and calm, with lots of black. Weak: the tower is small and its section detail is fine at 1:1. The beam is the largest element and draws the eye first. Sweep as foreshortening is a convention the player has to learn.

## Ranking (reviewer's, not the pick)

1. v2: reads at once as the object in the name, the calmest and most OP-XY of the five, and every control has its own clear part; it needs a stronger pulse cue.
2. v5: the most honest mapping of the sound (pulse bands in the beam) in one calm scene; it needs the tower drawn a little larger.
3. v1: the boldest image, and lens is literally the lens; sweep is expensive and the subject is less obvious to some players.
4. v3: a good mechanism drawing, but the clock splits the eye and the rings flirt with targets.
5. v4: the most atmospheric, but the busiest body, with a small subject and crossing hatches at the extremes.
