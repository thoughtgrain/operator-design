# Tide · subject pass 02 · notes

Pass 01 was turned down whole, so none of these goes back to traces, stacked plates, survey plans, cell grids or capture sections. Five sketches at 1:1 (`v1..v5/rest.png`, `rsvg-convert -w 480 -h 220 -b black`), all from `sketch.py`, each a 3D model put through one camera. Every frame lints 0 errors, 0 warnings, and has no text below the stock header. Each has one `#CDCDCD` element and is 89–95 % black. Uniqueness: `ready_for_pick`, no problems.

**v1 · exploded view.** One tuned hollow at large scale, running off the bottom edge. Its water is lifted out on the axis, with standing crests across it and the rim as the bright element. The inlet stone is drawn out of the lip's notch, and the swell's tongue comes in from the left edge.
At 1:1 the cup and lens are big, calm and clearly one object, and the exploded line explains "waves in, hollow rings". Weak: the lens looks like a lid, the tongue like a paddle, and the rock is thin (only the notch stone), so it can read as a product drawing.

**v2 · single-weight contour.** The tide itself: the swash's lace edge is the bright line running up the shore, with swell lines on the sea and strand marks below. A ledge of eight hollows sits in the swash.
At 1:1 it's the most literal and unmistakably shore. Weak: with one weight, value has to carry everything, so the many equal grey lines feel busy, the sea crowds the swash on the right, and the ledge reads as a pasted blob.

**v3 · hatched mass.** Eight stone pipes stand in an arc in the surge, cut to the partials, with hatching on their turned-away sides. The fundamental's mouth is the brightest edge.
At 1:1 it's crisp and physical, the hatching is clean at 3.6 px, and every control has a different part to move (crests, waterline, heights, lean). Weak: graduated cylinders sit close to a bar chart or organ, and it shows the model more than the sea.

**v4 · edge-lit silhouette.** Foam seen at water level: black bubble domes in eight sizes, lit only along their back edges. The largest bubble is the bright one. A bubble's ring is set by its size, so the foam *is* the resonator bank.
At 1:1 it's the calmest and most distinctive frame, it looks like nothing else in the library, and the edge light is very OP-XY. Weak: the step from foam to "tuned hollows" isn't obvious, the top half is empty, and the arcs could drift toward moons.

**v5 · detailed line art.** One boulder at the sea's edge with the pool in its worn top: hidden-line facets, the swell's fronts running in around it, and the pool's water as the bright ring.
At 1:1 the rock reads as a rock and the scene is clear and quiet. Weak: the pool is small and sits like a collar at the back of the top, one pool doesn't show eight resonators, and depth gets only ~20 px of travel.

## Ranking

1. v4: the most original and the most OP-XY looking, and the physics is honest (bubbles ring at their size). The subject needs one sentence of explanation, though.
2. v1: the clearest about what the machine does, and big and calm at 1:1, but the parts need more rock in them.
3. v3: crisp and every control has its own part, but it risks reading as a chart.
4. v5: a good scene, but the subject (the pool) is too small in it.
5. v2: literal and on-theme, but busy at 1:1 with one weight.

The pick is the owner's.
