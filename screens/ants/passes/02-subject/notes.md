# Ants · pass 02 · subject notes

Five new directions, none revisiting pass 01 (no orrery, elevation, survey plan, field-and-figure or formicarium) or the kept v1. All five are rest frames at the defaults (ants 4, scent 50, fade 30, scale pent), 480 × 220, stock header as the template has it, no text below it, grey only. Generator: `sketch.py` (v5 modelled in 3D and projected through one camera). Lint: 0 errors, 0 warnings on all five (no `text-in-drawing`); 87–92 % of the body black. Uniqueness: `design_pass_submit` → `ready_for_pick`, every pair differs in angle and language, subject overlap ≤ 0.16. Judged at 1:1 from `vN/rest.png`.

**v1 · rule as a real object · single-weight contour.** The colony walking a glockenspiel, one bar per degree; the loop the ants tread across the bars is the phrase.
Strong: instantly musical and physical, scale literally adds bars, calm and very black. Weak: without the ants it's any melodic machine; the oval loop doesn't show which bars sound.

**v2 · cutaway at work · hatched mass.** One big ant in side elevation, gaster cut open to show the gland and duct laying the trail, antennae reading it, followers on the trail behind.
Strong: the only one that explains the rule (deposit, sense, evaporate), and scent gets a clean rotation. Weak: shows the rule, not the phrase; a lot of hatching; followers only just read at 30 px; the slab row is close to a strip.

**v3 · the trace left behind · edge-lit silhouette.** A black leaf, margin and veins edge-lit, one vein per degree, the bright trail worn across it.
Strong: the prettiest, quietest frame. Weak: the leaf is arbitrary (veins as degrees has to be explained), and the self-crossing loop reads as a zigzag.

**v4 · one detail at large scale · detailed line art.** A twig fork, one branch per degree of the octave, a scout ant on the knot swinging onto the scented branch, ants out along it, old scent hatched on unused branches, the rest of the lattice past the frame.
Strong: one clear subject, the frame edge does real work, every control has a distinct channel (rotation, hatch reach, count, branch count), and it can only be ants. Weak: one decision, not the phrase over time; at chrom twelve branches crowd near the knot.

**v5 · the object in the name · hidden-line 3D.** Modelled ants (ellipsoid bodies, segmented gasters, jointed legs) walking the bright trail between two rows of degree stones, one camera.
Strong: literal ants, properly built and lit as a family with theseus. Weak: the busiest frame at 1:1 (legs and stones overlap), the costliest (pre-drawn heading variants per ant), and the near row runs off both sides.

## Ranking (the designer's view; the pick is the owner's)

1. v4: the strongest single picture and the most this-machine; the missing phrase could come back as the scented branch's ants landing in step.
2. v1: the clearest link from ants to notes, and the calmest frame; needs the landings drawn.
3. v2: the best explanation of the rule, but a diagram of an ant more than a picture of the music.
4. v5: literal and well modelled, but cluttered and expensive.
5. v3: lovely, but the leaf isn't the machine.
